# Create Account

Before you can use Qubase, you need to create an account. An account gives you access to Workspaces, Projects, Database Schemas, AI-powered features, and collaboration tools.

## What you'll need

Before creating your account, make sure you have:

- A valid email address
- A secure password
- Internet access
- A supported web browser

## Create your account

1. Open the **Qubase** sign-up page.
2. Click **Create Account**.
3. Enter the required information:

| Field | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| Full Name | Your display name in Qubase |
| Email Address | Used for sign in and notifications |
| Password | Your account password |
| Confirm Password | Must match your password |

4. Review the information.
5. Click **Create Account**.

## Verify your email

After registering, Qubase sends a verification email to the address you provided.

1. Open your inbox.
2. Find the verification email from Qubase.
3. Click **Verify Email**.
4. Return to Qubase and sign in.

Your account must be verified before you can access all platform features.

## What happens next?

After your account is verified, you can:

- Create your first Workspace
- Join an existing Workspace
- Create Projects
- Design Database Schemas
- Collaborate with team members

## Tips

- Use your organization email if you are working with a team.
- Choose a strong password to help protect your account.
- Keep your email address active so you can receive invitations and password recovery emails.

## Troubleshooting

### I didn't receive the verification email.

- Check your Spam or Junk folder.
- Confirm that you entered the correct email address.
- Request another verification email from the sign-in page.

### I already have an account.

Use the **Sign In** page instead of creating another account.
