# Notifications

Notifications keep you informed about important events across your Workspaces and Projects.

Instead of checking every project manually, Qubase notifies you when relevant activity occurs, helping you stay up to date with your team's work.

---

## Before you begin

Before receiving notifications, ensure that you have:

- Created a Qubase account
- Joined a Workspace or Project

> [!NOTE]
> The notifications you receive depend on your project activity, permissions, and notification preferences.

---

## Why Notifications?

Notifications help you:

- Stay informed about project activity
- Respond to team collaboration quickly
- Track important updates
- Avoid missing important discussions

---

## Notification types

Depending on your project configuration, notifications may include:

- New member invitations
- Project activity
- Comments
- Mentions
- Collaboration updates
- Other project-related events

The available notification types may vary depending on your workspace configuration.

---

## View notifications

To view your notifications:

1. Open Qubase.
2. Select the **Notifications** icon.
3. Review your latest activity.

Unread notifications remain available until viewed or marked as read.

---

## Notification information

Each notification may include:

| Property | Description |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Title | Short summary |
| Description | Details about the activity |
| Source | Related workspace or project |
| Time | When the activity occurred |
| Status | Read or Unread |

---

## Manage notifications

Depending on your account settings, you may be able to:

- View notifications
- Mark notifications as read
- Mark all notifications as read
- Clear notifications (when supported)

---

## Best practices

- Review notifications regularly.
- Open important notifications promptly.
- Keep your notification list organized.
- Respond to collaboration requests when appropriate.

> [!TIP]
> Notifications help you stay aware of important project activity without constantly checking every workspace.
