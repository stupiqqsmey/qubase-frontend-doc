# Frequently Asked Questions

This page answers common questions about Qubase.

If you cannot find the answer you are looking for, refer to the related documentation guides or contact your workspace administrator.

---

## General

### What is Qubase?

Qubase is an AI-powered platform for visual database modeling, database engineering, and team collaboration.

---

### Which database systems are supported?

The supported database systems depend on your current Qubase configuration.

Refer to the **Supported Databases** guide for the latest information.

---

### Can I use Qubase without creating a Workspace?

No.

A Workspace is required before creating Projects and Database Schemas.

---

### Can one Workspace contain multiple Projects?

Yes.

A Workspace can contain multiple Projects.

---

### Can one Project contain multiple Database Schemas?

Yes.

Projects can organize multiple Database Schemas.

---

## Visual Modeling

### Can I edit imported templates?

Yes.

After importing a template, you can modify tables, columns, relationships, and other supported database objects.

---

### Can I generate SQL from my database model?

If SQL generation is available in your project, you can generate SQL from supported database schemas.

---

### Can I generate DBML?

Yes.

Qubase supports generating DBML for supported database models.

---

## AI Engineering

### Can AI generate an ERD?

Yes.

Prompt to ERD allows you to generate an ERD by describing your database requirements in natural language.

---

### Can AI explain SQL?

Yes.

Database Explainer helps explain SQL statements and database structures in understandable language.

---

### Does AI automatically modify my database?

No.

AI provides suggestions and generated content for review. You should verify the results before applying changes.

---

## Collaboration

### Can multiple users edit the same project?

Yes.

If Real-Time Collaboration is available and users have the required permissions, multiple members can collaborate on the same project.

---

### How do I invite team members?

Open your Workspace or Project and use **Invite Members**.

---

### How do I manage project members?

Project Members allows authorized users to review and manage member access.

---

## Account

### How do I change my password?

Open **Account Settings** and navigate to **Password Management**.

---

### How do I update my profile?

Open your account menu and select **Profile**.

---

### Why can't I access a Workspace?

You may not have permission to access the selected Workspace.

Contact the Workspace Owner or Administrator if you believe access should be available.
