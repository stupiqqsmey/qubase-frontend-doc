# Authentication

Authentication verifies your identity before granting access to Qubase.

After successfully signing in, you can access your workspaces, projects, database schemas, and other features based on your assigned permissions.

Authentication helps protect your account and ensures that only authorized users can access project resources.

---

## Before you begin

Before signing in, ensure that you have:

- A registered Qubase account
- Valid sign-in credentials
- Internet access

> [!NOTE]
> Authentication is required before accessing Workspaces, Projects, and other protected resources.

---

## Sign in

To sign in:

1. Open the Qubase sign-in page.
2. Enter your account credentials.
3. Complete any required verification steps.
4. Select **Sign In**.

After successful authentication, you will be redirected to your dashboard.

---

## Session management

After signing in, Qubase creates an authenticated session.

Depending on your configuration, you may be able to:

- Stay signed in
- Sign out manually
- Access multiple workspaces
- Switch between projects

Session behavior depends on your account configuration.

---

## Authentication security

To help protect your account:

- Use a strong password.
- Do not share your credentials.
- Sign out from shared devices.
- Keep your recovery information up to date.

---

## Common issues

### Unable to sign in

Check that:

- Your credentials are correct.
- Your account is active.
- Your internet connection is available.

### Access denied

If you successfully sign in but cannot access a workspace or project, contact the workspace owner or administrator to verify your permissions.

---

## Best practices

- Use a unique password.
- Avoid signing in on public devices.
- Review your account activity regularly.
- Keep your account information current.

> [!TIP]
> Signing out after using a shared computer helps protect your account.
