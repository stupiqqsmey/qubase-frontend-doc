# Account & Security

Account & Security helps you manage your Qubase account, protect your information, and control access to workspaces and projects.

Whether you are working independently or collaborating with a team, understanding authentication and permissions helps ensure that your data remains secure.

---

## What is Account & Security?

This module covers the features that allow you to:

- Sign in securely
- Manage your profile
- Update your password
- Understand roles and permissions
- Control workspace access
- Manage project access

These features help protect your account while supporting secure collaboration.

---


## Benefits

Account & Security helps you:

---

## Best practices

- Use a strong password.
- Keep your profile information up to date.
- Review your access permissions regularly.
- Only grant permissions when necessary.

> [!TIP]
> Understanding your permissions helps prevent accidental changes and keeps collaboration secure.
