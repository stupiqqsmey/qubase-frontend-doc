# Workspace Permissions

Workspace Permissions determine what members are allowed to do within a Workspace.

Permissions help protect shared resources by ensuring that only authorized members can perform specific actions. Depending on your assigned role, you may have different levels of access.

---

## Before you begin

Before managing Workspace Permissions, ensure that you have:

- Access to a Workspace
- Permission to manage workspace members (if applicable)

> [!NOTE]
> Available permissions depend on your assigned role within the Workspace.

---

## What are Workspace Permissions?

Workspace Permissions control access to Workspace-level resources.

Examples include:

- Viewing the Workspace
- Updating Workspace information
- Creating Projects
- Managing Workspace members
- Viewing Workspace activity

Workspace Permissions apply across the entire Workspace.

---

## Permission workflow

```text
Workspace
      │
      ▼
Assigned Role
      │
      ▼
Workspace Permissions
      │
      ▼
Allowed Actions
```

---

## Managing Workspace access

Depending on your assigned role, you may be able to:

- View Workspace information
- Update Workspace details
- Create Projects
- Invite members
- Remove members
- Manage Workspace settings

Available actions depend on your assigned permissions.

---

## Why Workspace Permissions matter

Workspace Permissions help:

- Protect shared resources
- Organize team responsibilities
- Prevent unauthorized changes
- Improve collaboration
- Maintain Workspace security

---

## Best practices

- Grant only the permissions required.
- Review Workspace access regularly.
- Remove inactive members when appropriate.
- Keep Workspace membership organized.

> [!TIP]
> Assign permissions based on responsibilities rather than convenience.
