# Password Management

Password Management allows you to update your account password and maintain the security of your Qubase account.

Regularly updating your password and following good security practices helps protect your personal information, workspaces, and projects from unauthorized access.

---

## Before you begin

Before changing your password, ensure that you have:

- Signed in to your Qubase account
- Access to your current account credentials

> [!NOTE]
> Changing your password affects only your account. Your projects and workspaces remain unchanged.

---

## Change your password

To change your password:

1. Sign in to your Qubase account.
2. Open **Account Settings**.
3. Navigate to **Password Management**.
4. Enter your current password.
5. Enter your new password.
6. Confirm the new password.
7. Save your changes.

Your password is updated after successful verification.

---

## Forgot your password

If you cannot sign in because you forgot your password:

1. Open the sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Follow the password recovery instructions.
5. Create a new password.

After resetting your password, you can sign in using the updated credentials.

---

## Password requirements

For better security, your password should:

- Be at least the required minimum length
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include numbers
- Include special characters when supported
- Avoid common or easily guessed words

Password requirements may vary depending on your account configuration.

---

## Security recommendations

To keep your account secure:

- Use a unique password.
- Do not reuse passwords from other services.
- Do not share your password.
- Update your password if you believe it has been compromised.

---

## Benefits

Managing your password helps:

- Protect your account
- Prevent unauthorized access
- Improve account security
- Reduce security risks

---

## Best practices

- Use a strong and unique password.
- Update your password periodically.
- Store passwords securely.
- Sign out from shared devices after use.

> [!TIP]
> A strong password is one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect your Qubase account.
