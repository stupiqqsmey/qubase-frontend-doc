# User Profile

Your User Profile contains the personal information associated with your Qubase account.

Keeping your profile information accurate helps other team members identify you and improves collaboration across Workspaces and Projects.

---

## Before you begin

Before updating your profile, ensure that you have:

- Signed in to your Qubase account
- Permission to edit your profile

> [!NOTE]
> Profile changes affect only your personal account and do not modify project or workspace settings.

---

## What is a User Profile?

Your profile represents your identity within Qubase.

Depending on your account configuration, your profile may include:

- Display Name
- Email Address
- Profile Picture
- Account Information

The available profile fields may vary depending on your account configuration.

---

## View your profile

To view your profile:

1. Sign in to Qubase.
2. Open the account menu.
3. Select **Profile**.

Your current profile information is displayed.

---

## Update your profile

To update your profile:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Edit the available fields.
3. Review your changes.
4. Save the updated information.

Your profile is updated immediately after saving.

---

## Profile information

Your profile may contain:

| Property | Description |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Display Name | Your public name within Qubase |
| Email Address | Your account email |
| Profile Picture | Your account avatar (if supported) |
| Joined Date | Date your account was created |

---

## Benefits

Keeping your profile updated helps:

- Improve team collaboration
- Make your account easier to identify
- Keep account information accurate

---

## Best practices

- Use your real name or your team's naming convention.
- Keep your email address up to date.
- Review your profile information periodically.
- Update your profile whenever your account details change.

> [!TIP]
> A complete profile helps teammates recognize you more easily during collaboration.
